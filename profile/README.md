## 🌆 About the Game

**Pokémon Legends: Z-A** takes place in **Lumiose City**, a major city in the Kalos region. An urban redevelopment project is transforming the city, creating new spaces where people and Pokémon can coexist.

Players explore streets, shops, cafés, parks, wild zones, and other areas throughout Lumiose City while catching and training Pokémon. Hotel Z serves as the player's home base during the adventure.

Unlike traditional Pokémon RPGs, the game introduces **real-time battles**, allowing Trainers and Pokémon to move around the battlefield while attacks are performed according to timing and positioning.

## ⚔️ Real-Time Gameplay

One of the biggest changes in **Pokémon Legends: Z-A** is its real-time battle system.

Instead of waiting for traditional turn-based commands, players can move their Trainer and Pokémon around the battlefield. Pokémon perform their moves when commanded, meaning positioning, timing, attack range, and area of effect can become important during combat.

This creates a faster style of Pokémon combat where players need to react to opponents and carefully decide when to attack, dodge, switch Pokémon, or use special abilities.

## 🏆 Z-A Royale

The **Z-A Royale** is a major competition in the game. Each night, battle zones appear where Trainers can compete against one another.

During the day, players can explore wild zones, catch Pokémon, train their team, and prepare for the next competition.

The objective is to climb through the ranks from **Rank Z to Rank A**. Bonus card challenges can also provide additional objectives during battles.

## 🔥 Mega Evolution

Mega Evolution returns as an important feature in **Pokémon Legends: Z-A**.

When the bond between a Trainer and Pokémon is strong enough, a Key Stone and Mega Stone can activate Mega Evolution. A Pokémon temporarily gains increased power and may even change its appearance or typing.

The game features numerous Mega-Evolved Pokémon, making Mega Evolution an important part of team building and combat strategy.
